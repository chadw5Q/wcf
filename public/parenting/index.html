Intentional Parenting

"Above all else guard yourheart, for it is the wellspring of life. - Proverbs 4:23
The Central Idea
Behavior is never the real issue — the heart is.
Children are worshippers by design. They will bow to something. Our job is to point that worship toward God — not just manage what shows on the outside.

The 4 T's of Communication

How to Talk to Your Kids About Hard Things

T1

Trust

Build the relationship before the conversation. Kids talk to people they trust.

T2

Timing

6–12 months before they're likely to encounter it. It's never too late to start.

T3

Tone

Receptive, informal, composed. 60–70% of communication is non-verbal.

T4

Talk

Have the conversation. Proactive beats reactive every time.
